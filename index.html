02

Information we collect

Content you provide

  • Text you type or paste to be rewritten in a different tone, including text shared into ToneFix from other apps via the share sheet.
  • Voice input for the Voice-to-Tone feature — your speech is transcribed to text; ToneFix does not keep the audio recording itself.
  • Custom tones, saved snippets, and favorites you create.
  • Feedback messages you submit through "Report a Bug" or "Request a Feature" — these include your message text, app version, and platform, but no email address unless you choose to include one in the message itself.

Automatically collected

  • An anonymous device identifier, created via Firebase Anonymous Authentication the first time you open the app. It isn't linked to your name, email, or any other account you may hold.
  • App diagnostics — crash reports and performance data (see Third-party services).
  • Local usage statistics — which tones you use and how often — kept only on your device to power the in-app Analytics screen. This never leaves your phone.

What we deliberately don't collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your contacts, photos, or files, beyond text you explicitly share into the app
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Browsing history outside the app

04

How rewriting works (Google Gemini)

When you tap rewrite, the message text (and, for Voice-to-Tone, the transcribed text) is sent directly to Google's Gemini API for processing, along with the tone you selected. The rewritten result is returned to the app and shown to you.

Google processes this text under its own Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We don't control how long Google retains API requests for abuse-monitoring purposes; we encourage you to review Google's terms if you plan to rewrite sensitive text.

We recommend avoiding highly sensitive personal information (medical, financial, or otherwise confidential details) in text you submit for rewriting, the same way you would with any cloud AI service.

05

How your data is stored

If you choose to save a rewrite, mark something as a favorite, or create a custom tone or snippet, that item is stored in Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase, operated by Google) under your anonymous device account. It is not visible to other users and is not linked to a real-world identity.

Your local usage statistics (which tones you use, daily streaks) stay entirely on your device in local storage — they're never uploaded anywhere, including to us.

06

Notifications

ToneFix can send two kinds of notifications, both of which you control from Settings → Daily Reminder:

  • Scheduled reminders (e.g. a daily nudge, a feature tip) generated on your device.
  • Push notifications delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging, which requires a device token to be registered with Google's servers so a message can be routed to your device. This token is not linked to your identity.

08

Retention & deletion

  • You can delete all saved history at any time from Settings → Clear All History. This is permanent and immediate.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all local data collection and cancels any scheduled reminders on that device.
  • To request deletion of data associated with your anonymous account that isn't covered by the in-app controls above, contact us using the details in Contact us — include your app version and roughly when you used the app, since we have no name or email to look you up by otherwise.
  • Crash diagnostics sent to Sentry are retained according to Sentry's own data retention settings on our account, typically a limited number of days.
09

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA). Because ToneFix doesn't collect your name or email, most of these rights are already exercisable directly in the app:

  • Access / delete — Settings → Clear All History, or uninstall the app.
  • Notifications — turn off anytime in Settings → Daily Reminder, or via your device's system notification settings.
  • Microphone — revoke at any time in Android Settings → Apps → ToneFix → Permissions.

For anything not covered above, reach out via Contact us.

10

Children's privacy

ToneFix is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11

Security

Data in transit to Google's Gemini API and Firebase services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore access is restricted by security rules scoped to your anonymous account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

12

International data transfers

Google's Gemini API, Firebase, and Sentry may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. By using ToneFix, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations.
